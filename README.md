# 6TH-Season-of-Indian-Karting-Race-Go-Kart-Design-Competition

## Objective
This project covers my team's participation in the Final Round of the 6th Season of the Indian Karting Race "IKR", organized by ISIEINDIA at Galgotias University, Gr. Noida, from 11 to 15 January 2022. We represented **Mar Athanasius College of Engineering** in a national-level student karting competition, where teams are judged on engineering, validation and performance. The event gave me hands-on exposure to electric vehicle design principles and to working in a team on a real vehicle under competition conditions.

## Highlights

- ✔ Participated in the **Final Round** of the 6th Season of Indian Karting Race, organized by ISIEINDIA
- ✔ **Cleared the in-house technical inspection**
- ✔ Gained exposure to **electric vehicle design principles**
- ✔ Developed **team collaboration, problem-solving and engineering validation** skills
- ✔ Represented **Mar Athanasius College of Engineering**

### Skills Learned

- Understanding of electric vehicle design principles.
- Preparing a vehicle to meet technical inspection requirements.
- Engineering validation: checking that a design works and meets the rules.
- Team collaboration and problem-solving in a competition setting.
- Working under event deadlines and compliance requirements.

## Steps

Below are the key stages of the competition:

### 1. Team Preparation
The team prepared for the competition around the event's technical requirements, applying electric vehicle design principles and working together on problem-solving.

### 2. In-House Technical Inspection
Before racing, the vehicle went through the in-house technical inspection to verify that it met the safety and design requirements. **The team cleared this inspection.**

### 3. Engineering Validation
The team validated its engineering decisions through checks and testing, which built practical problem-solving and teamwork skills.

### 4. Final Round
The team took part in the Final Round of the competition at Galgotias University over five days.
